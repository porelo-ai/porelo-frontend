**What must be true.** A person setting a machine up with the wizard can choose which provider the chat beside it
answers through, for example another agent or another model than the one they signed in, and sees which one answers.

The setup wizard with its chat is planned so that the chat uses the agent the person signed in; choosing another
provider was left out of that plan on purpose and kept as this idea, with low priority.

## To be checked

- Which providers the window can offer the chat: the agents porelo knows on the machine, or providers of their own.
- What the person is told of what goes to the chosen provider, as the planned chat already tells of the signed-in one.
