**Why this size.** a button in the dialog of creating, on Core's own answer.

**What must be true.** A person about to create a Porelo organization can ask what that needs at the address they typed, and reads each need with what to do, before anything is filed. In the foo test creating stopped at its end, on things that could be said before it.

## The shape

- *What does creating it need?* in the dialog of creating, on `porelo org create ADDRESS --check --json`.
- Each need shows Core's `words` and `next`, marked by its `state`: the window reads no more meaning in them.
- It does not hold *Create it* back: Core's own creating refuses what it must.

## Acceptance

- Asking shows each need's words and next step, and files nothing.
- A changed address drops the answer; a late answer of the old address is not shown.
- A refusal is said in its words.

## Decided while building

- **It is asked on a press,** not as the address is typed: asking may reach the forge.
- **A shut vault reads as Core says,** `cannot-tell` with its next step, as a window has no terminal to ask at.
