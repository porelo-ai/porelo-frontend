# F391 — Who Signs Comes From The Organization

**Status:** Idea.

**What must be true.** A person who signs a commit to a project's access file from the window signs with their own key and has it accepted by the signers their organization names, read from the organization, not from a daemon method that does not exist.

## Why

The window asks this computer's daemon for `ProjectSigners` before it signs or accepts an access commit, and no daemon serves that method: who may sign is the organization's to say, no longer a project file's. The window therefore refuses every signed access commit today, saying Porelo cannot say yet who signs, and the live check of the served contract is red on that name.

## Open questions

- What the window must show of who may sign, and to whom, so the organization's reading can be asked for it.
