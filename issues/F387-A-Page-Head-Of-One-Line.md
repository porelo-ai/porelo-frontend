# F387 — A Page Head Of One Line

**Status:** Review.

**What must be true.** What is important stands in sight, and the block above the lists no longer pushes it out. The rule: a normal state is not described, a departure is.

## The shape

**One line as the head of *Organization*:** the name, an `i` beside it, a warning if there is one, on the right "read <1 min ago", refresh if it is not elsewhere, and the menu.

- *What is the Porelo organization?* becomes the `i`, opening the explanation.
- *Choose another...* and *Create another...* go into the menu.
- *Kept in the GitHub organization...* and *keys vouched...* go into the explanation or a folded *About this organization* block below.
- *My card* and *you are an organization owner* go, since *People* and *Organization owners* say them. A card that is not right shows as a warning.
- **A warning never folds away:** *only owner, no backup key* stays in that line, short, and leads to *Organization owners*.

**Every page's explanation** is just an `i` beside the title, opening the explanation. It stands open once, the first time that page is opened; after that it stays folded unless opened, kept across a restart.

**On *Work*** the explanation belongs to the page, not to *List*: its `i` stands in the page's head beside the title, above *List* and *Running*, and can be reached from both. Opened, it stands above that switch.

## Acceptance

- *Organization*'s lists start right under one line, in a normal state and with a warning.
- The warning stays in sight and leads to *Organization owners*.
- Each page's explanation stands open the first time only, also after a restart, and its `i` opens it again.

## Decided while building

- *Organization* is no longer a lazy list: a lazy one drops the explanation while it is read, and the warning could not reach *Organization owners* far down. All its lists are built at once.
- The warning opens *Organization owners* when folded, and scrolls to it.
- An explanation folded in the version before counts as seen.
- *Projects on my machines*, reached from *My projects* with a way back, has no explanation of its own, as before: *My projects* explains it.

- **The `i` on a list's own title** on *My machines*, *My forges* and *My projects*, and in a head of its own on *Work* and *Needs you*, which have no such title.
- **"Kept in ..." and "keys vouched ..." go into the explanation,** in its *Where it is kept*.
- **An explanation opened stands until the page is left,** or closed; only the first opening is its own.
- **A menu for *Choose another...* and *Create another...*,** and a refresh, as the page had none.
